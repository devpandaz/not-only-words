> 金魚鉢  
> 割れて流れる  
> もののあり  
> THE GOLDFISH BOWL  
> HAS CRACKED AND IS FLOWING

さよならは  
言ぬものなり  
桜舞う  
Farewell is something that's not said  
Like the dance of cherry blossoms

二十回目の三月  
君と会う  
In my twentieth March  
I met you

夕虹や  
君に言いたいことが ある  
An evening rainbow  
I have something to tell you

熱風のかけらを  
君の手の中へ  
A piece of my great passion  
Sent into your hand

全霊で叫ぶよ  
夏果ての君に  
I'll scream with all of my might  
To you, during summer's end

> 聞きづらい  
> 声の持ちき  
> 夏探し  
> The one with the voice that's hard to hear  
> is looking for summer
